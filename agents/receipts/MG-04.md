## Behaviour rules covered

SPEC v1.1 amendment 11 (the wire contract, "pixels outside the mask equal the input") and amendment 14 (the evaluation gate, and provenance on the result):

- **Amendment 11, outside the mask bit for bit:** `pixels_outside_the_mask_come_back_bit_for_bit` (8-bit and 16-bit, RGB and RGBA; a 16-bit untouched pixel keeps its 16-bit value),
  `alpha_inside_the_mask_moves_towards_opaque_and_outside_it_does_not_move`, and the outside-the-mask loop in `lama_takes_the_object_out_and_leaves_the_rest_alone`.
- **Blend by the original mask only, never the dilated one:** `a_full_mask_takes_the_fill_and_a_soft_one_blends_between`, `the_model_mask_is_dilated_by_four_pixels_at_512_scale` (the dilation is what the
  model is shown, and `blend` is checked against the raw mask).
- **Crop, pad, square, clamp:** `the_mask_box_is_the_smallest_rectangle_over_the_selection`, `the_padding_is_32_pixels_or_a_quarter_of_the_longer_side`, `the_crop_pads_the_box_and_grows_it_to_a_square`,
  `the_crop_is_clamped_to_the_image_and_still_covers_the_selection`.
- **Resize:** `a_resize_lands_on_the_size_asked_for_and_a_flat_area_stays_flat`, `the_model_planes_are_512_square_and_come_back_at_the_crop_size`.
- **Stages (the MG-05 part):** `the_stages_run_in_order_and_say_which_one_they_are` - progress `0.0, 0.2, 0.25, 0.3, 0.9, 1.0` with messages prepare, prepare, load model, run, finish, finish - and at the
  queue level `a_lama_job_goes_through_its_stages_and_answers_with_the_model`, which also asserts the fake backend's sentence never appears on a LaMa job and that progress never goes backwards.
- **Cancel (the MG-05 part):** `a_cancelled_job_stops_at_the_stage_it_has_reached`, `a_lama_job_cancelled_while_queued_never_loads_the_model`, `a_lama_job_cancelled_while_running_stops_the_run`
  (the stand-in session is asked to stop mid-run, and the job ends `cancelled`), `an_empty_mask_returns_the_input_unchanged_and_never_loads_the_model`.
- **Order:** `two_lama_jobs_finish_in_the_order_they_arrived` extends MG-01's queue rather than duplicating it.
- **Session is lazy and kept:** `the_session_is_loaded_once_and_kept_for_the_next_job` (one load, two runs), and `a_model_that_is_installed_and_acknowledged_is_offered_without_loading_it` proves

- **The golden fixture is synthetic.** The prompt asks for a public-domain or CC0 Wikimedia Commons photo; there was no network, so nothing could be fetched, and the fixture was generated from the recipe in
  `crates/caim/tests/fixtures/SOURCE.md`. Every property the gate checks holds, but it proves LaMa behaves on a synthetic disc on a gradient, not on a photograph. Erik should drop in a real photo (the
  recipe is in `SOURCE.md`) before this gate is trusted.

  run and the compare links below are the handoff. `git status` is clean in both repos.
- **`ort` is a release candidate** (`2.0.0-rc.14` is the newest 2.x on crates.io). The requirement pins the rc exactly; revisit when 2.0.0 stable lands, and expect the `ort::session::RunOptions` / tensor
  API to move between rc's.
- **512 px limit.** A hole much wider than 512 px is downscaled into the window and comes back softer. Accepted for M1 and said so in the README; tiling is CAIM-13.
- **The result carries no model version or SHA-256.** Amendment 11's wire shape has only `model`, `device`, `provider`, `elapsed_ms`, and adding fields needs a SPEC edit first. B16 (the model pinned to the
  layer) waits for U04.
- **One worker thread, one session.** MG-01's single `caim-jobs` worker runs jobs one at a time, and `Pipeline` holds the session for the length of a run, so two runs could not overlap even with more
  workers. Priority lanes and latest-wins are CAIM-12.
- **16-bit fill precision:** untouched 16-bit pixels keep their exact value; the fill inside the mask is 8-bit precision scaled up, which the prompt accepts.
- **`byteorder`'s Unlicense leg and `webpki-root-certs`' CDLA-Permissive-2.0** are flagged above for Erik, in the same way `webpki-roots` was in MG-02.
- **MSRV is still declared (`rust-version = "1.95"`) and untested in CI.** `ort-sys` declares 1.92, so nothing new broke it, but nothing proves it either.
- PhotoCraft `agents/FEATURES.md` has no row for the inference runner itself, so nothing there was ticked; MG-04 and MG-05 in `agents/TASKS.md` are the tracking, and both are set to `review` on
  `ai/mg-04-status`. CAIM-13 (tiling) and B16 stay open.
